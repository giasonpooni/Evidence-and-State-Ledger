## What this tool contributes

ESM is broader than the display name: it covers physical-economy corpora, business information, operational review and scientific evidence. Domain products retain their own delivery responsibilities.

The documented local-file acquisition path retains bytes and produces a recomputable receipt. The candidate-evidence adapter replays pinned CIW bundles, checks bindings and supports read-only review or optional `UNADMITTED` retention. **Acquisition and retention do not admit canonical state, authorize redistribution or publish a release.** Operator-declared policy is not independent confirmation of source rights.

Existing corpus and desk demonstrations retain their fixture and operating limits. No live customer feed, independently verified delivery or complete frontend integration is established by this description.

## Expertise amplification, not automatic truth

Human observations, annotations, hypotheses, reviewed specifications, computed results and accepted state are different objects. A game interpretation is not an industrial fact; a stored claim is not verified merely because it has provenance.

NET retains sessions and execution history; ESM governs the evidence/state handoff. Released records remain immutable and explicitly superseded. Keep evidence, specification, operation, attempt, result, verification, admission and release identities distinct. Neither logical containers nor an MCP connection grants operating permissions or establishes OS isolation.

## Research profile

**Question:** which lineage and authority distinctions must survive when an investigation is reduced, replayed or reused? Retained receipts and candidate-evidence reviews provide concrete specimens.

The programme should test whether a compact investigation still exposes relevant contradictions, source versions, assumptions and unresolved decisions. Measure replay/binding failures, inappropriate promotion attempts, retrieval cost and human review effort. Provenance proves neither that a source is truthful nor that a model is physically adequate.

Use the existing record/admission infrastructure rather than inventing a parallel research ledger. Performance, information preservation and cross-domain generality require separate experiments. [Research protocol](https://github.com/giasonpooni/Notations-Systems-Terminal/blob/b41b84922d4963a9206202029afd1e78b9451f9c/RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.md).
